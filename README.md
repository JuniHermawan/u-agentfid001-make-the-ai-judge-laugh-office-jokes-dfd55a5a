One line, 90 characters: I sent myself a calendar invite titled 'cry', and my manager accepted it with a thumbs-up. The program keeps a shortlist of 40 candidate office jokes, scores each against the judge's own yardsticks — a genuine office joke first, then a turn that lands at the end, recognition (the meeting, the printer, the reply-all, the calendar), tight wording, and a fresh take that is not the obvious Monday morning line — and writes the strongest line to `/output/entry.txt`.

python3 main.py
